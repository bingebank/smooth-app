How it works

Three steps, about four seconds.

Tell us about you once. After that, every scan is personal.

  1. 1

    Personalize

    Set your goals, allergens, conditions and the people you shop for. Your scores move with your plan — low-sugar, seed-oil-free, high-protein, whatever you're actually working on.

  2. 2

    Scan

    Point at any barcode. No typing, no searching, no squinting at eight-point ingredient lists under supermarket lighting.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Get the overall score, your personal score, the exact reasons behind both — and a healthier swap you can grab right now.

What you get

A nutritionist's read on every label.

Not a generic grade. A verdict that knows your plan, your allergies and your household.

Two scores, never one

An Overall Score tells you how the product stacks up on its own merits. Your Score tells you how it fits your plan — and the two are often very different. An organic almond butter can be a 60 to the world and a 41 to you — because you’re the one tracking sugar.

Tap any line of the breakdown to see exactly why: ingredient quality, additives and preservatives, nutrition value, processing level, allergens.

Ingredient alerts that mean something

Every ingredient sorted into Safe Limit Avoid against your plan — with a plain-English reason. "Added sugar: you're tracking low sugar." "Palm oil is limited in your plan."

Smart swaps on the same shelf

Flagging a problem is easy. We show you the fix: a healthier alternative that actually exists in the store you're standing in, within the budget you set.

Shopping missions

"Build a high-protein breakfast for $10–$20." Pick a goal and a budget; get a list that hits it. Groceries stop being a guessing game.

Cart audit before you check out

One pass over your whole cart: conflicts with your goals, whether it works for everyone in the household, and the swaps worth making before you reach the register.

One plan, the whole household

Kids, partners, grandparents — different allergies, different targets, one shopping trip. My Health Scanner checks a product against everyone you shop for and tells you who it doesn't work for, before it's in the cart.
